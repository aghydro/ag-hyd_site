Research

What we do

By integrating advanced hydrological and cropping system models with in-situ and remote sensing data, we explore the complex linkages between soil physical health, water quantity and quality, and crop production. Our work spans across scales ranging from the root zone to large watersheds, developing data-driven strategies to optimize agricultural management, protect water quality, and enhance sustainability of agroecosystems.

Soil moisture dynamics

Mapping how water moves through the root zone under different tillage and cover-crop practices, at field and regional scale.

Groundwater–surface water interactions

Quantifying exchange between aquifers and streams to understand irrigation's downstream effects on river flow.

Climate-smart irrigation scheduling

Building decision tools that tell growers when and how much to irrigate, cutting water use without cutting yield.

Remote sensing of crop water use

Using satellite and drone imagery to estimate evapotranspiration across thousands of fields at once.

Watershed hydrologic modeling

Coupling land-use and climate scenarios to project how future water availability will shape farming regions.

Sediment and nutrient losses

Tracking how agricultural practices influence the movement of sediments and nutrients from fields into local water bodies.
