  photo-4.jpg

Recommended: landscape orientation, at least 720x520px, JPG format,
each under ~500KB (compress with squoosh.app or similar so the page
stays fast). Once a file is added, the "Photo coming soon" placeholder
for that slot disappears automatically — no HTML/CSS changes needed.

Want more or fewer than 6 photos, or a different naming pattern?
Edit partials/gallery.html and update css/styles.css (.gallery-track
animation) accordingly, or just ask.
